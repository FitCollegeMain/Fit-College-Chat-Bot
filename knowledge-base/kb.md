## 1. SIS30321 Certificate III in Fitness — Gym Instructor

**Overview:** Entry-level gym qualification and the starting point for a personal-training career. Graduates work as Gym Instructors, Group Fitness Instructors or Outdoor Group Instructors and can register with AUSactive.

**Study Time:** Allowable timeframe 12 months (extensions available). Typical online completion ~6 months. Campus full-time ~8 weeks; part-time ~15 weeks.

**Study Load:** Nominal hours 1242; recommended ~27 hours/week to finish within the standard timeframe. Campus full-time 12 contact hours/week over 7 weeks (Mon–Thu, 10am–1pm); part-time 6 hours/week over 14 weeks (evenings).

**Assessment / Test Load:** 8 topics — First Aid; Anatomy & Physiology; Screening & Customer Service; Healthy Nutrition; Fitness Assessments; Design & Deliver Sessions; Deliver Group Exercise; Professional Development. Mix of knowledge quizzes, short-answer questions, interactive online tasks and practical assessments (observed in person or by uploaded video). Includes First Aid + CPR competency.

**Entry Requirements:** No formal prerequisites. Minimum age 14. LLN at ACSF Level 3 (around Year 10). International: IELTS 5.5 (no band below 5.0) or equivalent.

**Study Modes & Intakes:** Online/Virtual Campus (enrol anytime, start within 72 hours); campus full-time or part-time. Campus intakes February, May, September. Student-visa version CRICOS 110301E (20 hrs/week blended, ~22 weeks; intakes Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct at Gold Coast, Brisbane, Melbourne, Sydney).

**Pricing & Funding:** Industry price range ~$1,300–$8,150 (confirm current price). Interest-free payment plans. QLD Certificate 3 Guarantee subsidy: co-contribution $1,750 concessional / $2,000 non-concessional (eligibility applies).

**Career Outcomes:** Gym Instructor, Group Fitness Instructor, Outdoor Group Instructor. Register with AUSactive once qualified.

**Pathways:** Into SIS40221 Certificate IV in Fitness (commonly bundled as FIT Elite). University: Bachelor of Exercise Science, Sport Coaching, Exercise Physiology, Sports Management, Sports Nutrition.

**FAQs:**
- *Do I need any experience to start?* No — there are no prerequisites. It's entry-level; you just need to be at least 14 and meet basic language, literacy and numeracy requirements.
- *Can I finish faster than 6 months?* Yes. The timeframe is an allowance, not a minimum — motivated online students often finish sooner. Campus full-time runs about 8 weeks.
- *Is this enough to become a personal trainer?* Cert III qualifies you as a gym/group instructor. To train clients one-on-one as a PT you also need Cert IV — many students bundle both.

## 2. SIS40221 Certificate IV in Fitness — Personal Trainer

**Overview:** The qualification that makes you a Personal Trainer, able to work independently or self-employed. Builds on Certificate III and lets you register with AUSactive as a PT.

**Study Time:** Allowable timeframe 12 months (extensions). Typical online completion ~6 months. Campus full-time ~8 weeks; part-time ~15 weeks.

**Study Load:** Nominal hours 1242; ~27 hours/week. Campus full-time ~12 contact hours/week over 7 weeks; part-time ~6 hours/week over 14 weeks. Includes a 4-week applied Behaviour Change Project.

**Assessment / Test Load:** 9 topics — Movement Assessment & Prescription; Effective Personal Training; Nutrition & Behaviour Change; Body Composition Training; Training Adolescent Clients (13–17); Training Older Clients (55+); Develop Business Proposals; Behaviour Change Project (4-week); Provide CPR. Quizzes, short-answer, online tasks and practicals (in person or by video). Current CPR certificate must be uploaded.

**Entry Requirements:** Must hold/be competent in the Cert III core units, including a current HLTAID011 Provide First Aid (within 3 years), HLTWHS001 and the SISFFIT screening/assessment/group units. Working With Children positive notification. LLN at ACSF Level 4 (around Year 12). International: IELTS 5.5 (no band below 5.0) or equivalent.

**Study Modes & Intakes:** Online/Virtual Campus, or campus full-time/part-time. Campus intakes February, May, September. Student-visa version CRICOS 110302D (25 hrs/week blended, ~22 weeks).

**Pricing & Funding:** Industry price range ~$1,200–$8,350 (confirm current price). Interest-free payment plans.

**Career Outcomes:** Personal Trainer in commercial gyms, PT studios, outdoor group training, online/virtual coaching, corporate wellness, sport & rec centres. Specialise in functional training, S&C, HIIT, body composition, lifestyle coaching, or run your own PT business.

**Pathways:** Bachelor of Exercise Science and related degrees. Combine with the Diploma of Sport or S&C to specialise.

**FAQs:**
- *Can I do Cert IV without Cert III?* You need the Cert III core units first (including a current first aid certificate). If you don't have them, start with Cert III — or take both together via FIT Elite.
- *Does it cover running my own business?* Yes — there's a dedicated topic on developing business proposals, so you can set up as a self-employed PT.

## 3. FIT Elite Personal Trainer (Cert III + IV bundle)

**Overview:** The flagship advanced PT package. Combines Certificate III and Certificate IV in Fitness with advanced skill sets, so graduates leave ahead of the competition.

**Study Time:** Covers both Cert III and Cert IV timeframes; allowable up to 12 months per qualification. Any study mode.

**Study Load:** Two full qualifications plus up to 50 Continuing Education Credits (CECs) — a higher overall load than a single certificate.

**Assessment / Test Load:** All Cert III and Cert IV assessments, PLUS 30-CEC Advanced Skills (Boxing + Suspension + Kettlebell), PLUS the chosen specialisation stream's assessments.

**Entry Requirements:** As per Certificate III (entry level) — the bundle includes Cert III, so no prior fitness qualification is required.

**Study Modes:** Available online or on campus.

**Pricing & Funding:** Premium bundle pricing — confirm current price and any EOFY offer. Interest-free payment plans.

**Career Outcomes:** Advanced Personal Trainer with extra specialisations in boxing, suspension and kettlebell training, plus either strength & conditioning coaching or NDIS disability support.

**Pathways:** Choose one stream — ASCA Level 1 accredited Strength & Conditioning, OR the NDIS Disability Skill Set (CHCSS00130). Leads into degree pathways.

**FAQs:**
- *What's included over a normal Cert IV?* Cert III + Cert IV, advanced skills in boxing, suspension and kettlebell (30 CECs), and your choice of an S&C stream or the NDIS disability skill set.
- *Which specialisation should I pick?* Choose the S&C stream to coach athletes and sport performance; choose the disability skill set to add NDIS support work and a second income stream.

## 4. SIS50321 Diploma of Sport — Coaching

**Overview:** Qualifies you as a sports coach and development professional for clubs, academies and national governing bodies. Includes ASCA Level 1 strength & conditioning coursework.

**Study Time:** Online and self-paced. Confirm current allowable timeframe.

**Study Load:** Self-paced online; substantial practical coaching — you deliver 24 sessions across three sports/groups, plus ASCA Level 1 practical tasks.

**Assessment / Test Load:** Four areas — Start-up activities; Strength & Conditioning (meets ASCA L1; six practical tasks signed off by an ASCA-accredited coach L1+); Plan & Conduct Sport Programs (24 sessions across 3 sports/groups); Sport Integrity & Leadership (WHS/risk, anti-doping, integrity, leadership/mentoring).

**Entry Requirements:** Requires access to a community group or sporting organisation to coach participants. Confirm LLN requirements.

**Study Modes:** Online, self-paced.

**Pricing & Funding:** Confirm current price and payment plans.

**Career Outcomes:** Sports Coach, mentor, or sports development professional at clubs, academies and governing bodies.

**Pathways:** Credit into Bond University Bachelor of Sport Management and Bachelor of Business. ASCA registration on completion (membership fees apply). Partnership with Tennis Australia.

**FAQs:**
- *Do I need a team to coach already?* Yes — you'll need access to a community group or sporting organisation to deliver and be assessed on real coaching sessions.
- *Does it count towards a university degree?* Yes — it carries credit into Bond University's Sport Management and Business degrees.

## 5. Strength & Conditioning (ASCA Level 1 Accredited)

**Overview:** A standalone course that certifies you as a Strength & Conditioning coach, accredited by ASCA, the national S&C peak body.

**Study Time:** Allowable timeframe 6 months. Online only.

**Study Load:** Online with mandatory practical hours built into the assessment tasks. Worth 20 AUSactive CPD points.

**Assessment / Test Load:** 14 quizzes, 5 online tasks and 5 practical assessments (screening, fitness testing, risk assessment, session delivery, plus ASCA-signed coaching tasks covering barbell lifts, speed/agility, energy-system conditioning, flexibility/mobility and youth coaching).

**Entry Requirements:** Requires access to a community group or sporting organisation to coach participants.

**Study Modes:** Online, self-paced.

**Pricing & Funding:** Confirm current price and payment plans.

**Career Outcomes:** Certified Strength & Conditioning Coach working with athletes and sports performance.

**Pathways:** On completion, submit your Statement of Attainment to ASCA and pay the annual membership to register. Also forms the S&C component of the Diploma of Sport.

**FAQs:**
- *Is this the same as the Diploma's S&C part?* Yes — it mirrors the strength & conditioning component of the Diploma of Sport, offered as a focused standalone course.

## 6. CHCSS00130 Individual Support — Disability Skill Set (NDIS)

**Overview:** An NDIS-focused skill set that qualifies you to work as a disability support worker. Designed to help fitness professionals diversify their income with support work.

**Study Time:** Online. Confirm current allowable timeframe.

**Study Load:** Online with virtual and phone support. Worth 15 AUSactive CPD points.

**Assessment / Test Load:** Three units — CHCDIS011 (ongoing skills development, strengths-based approach); CHCDIS012 (community participation and social inclusion); CHCDIS020 (work effectively in disability support).

**Entry Requirements:** Confirm entry and any screening requirements (e.g. police/WWCC checks).

**Study Modes:** Online.

**Pricing & Funding:** Confirm current price and payment plans.

**Career Outcomes:** Disability Support Worker, including NDIS participant support. Person-centred support roles.

**Pathways:** Graduate job pathway via FIT College's partner, FITCare Support Services (selection process applies).

**Other Notes:** Market context — the NDIS National Workforce Plan projects around 353,000 workers needed for ~530,000 participants.

**FAQs:**
- *Can I combine this with fitness work?* Yes — it's designed so fitness professionals can add disability support as a second income stream.
- *Is there help finding a job after?* FIT College partners with FITCare Support Services for graduate job opportunities, subject to a selection process.

## 7. HLTAID011 Provide First Aid (PFA)

**Overview:** A nationally recognised first aid course covering the treatment of a range of first aid scenarios, including CPR.

**Study Time:** 1 day (approximately 6 hours), face-to-face.

**Assessment / Test Load:** Scenario-based practical assessment plus the CPR competency (covers HLTAID009) — burns, asthma, anaphylaxis, fractures and more, with CPR to current ARC guidelines.

**Entry Requirements:** No prerequisites.

**Study Modes:** Face-to-face delivery.

**Pricing & Funding:** Confirm current price.

**Career Outcomes:** Meets first aid requirements for fitness, coaching and many workplaces. Required for Cert IV entry.

**Other Notes:** NRT-approved. Certificate typically valid 3 years (CPR component 12 months).

**FAQs:**
- *Does this include CPR?* Yes — Provide First Aid includes the CPR competency (HLTAID009).

## 8. HLTAID009 Provide CPR

**Overview:** A short, nationally recognised CPR course to current ARC guidelines.

**Study Time:** Approximately 2 hours, face-to-face.

**Assessment / Test Load:** Practical CPR demonstration to current ARC guidelines.

**Entry Requirements:** No prerequisites.

**Study Modes:** Face-to-face delivery.

**Pricing & Funding:** Confirm current price.

**Career Outcomes:** Keeps your CPR current — required and must be kept up to date for Cert IV in Fitness.

**Other Notes:** CPR certification is generally renewed every 12 months.

**FAQs:**
- *How often do I need to renew CPR?* Generally every 12 months, which is why many trainers refresh it annually.

## 9. TAE40122 Certificate IV in Training and Assessment

**Overview:** Qualifies you as a trainer and assessor in the vocational education (VET) sector — the qualification RTOs require to deliver and assess nationally recognised training.

**Study Time:** Online or on campus. Three intakes per year. Confirm current timeframe.

**Assessment / Test Load:** Assessment across the TAE40122 units of competency (training design, delivery and assessment). Delivered under FC Education.

**Entry Requirements:** Aimed at anyone wanting to train or assess in the VET sector — not just fitness. Confirm vocational competency requirements.

**Study Modes:** Online or on campus; three intakes per year.

**Pricing & Funding:** Confirm current price and payment plans.

**Career Outcomes:** VET trainer/assessor at an RTO or workplace training role.

**Pathways:** Upgrade pathway available from TAE40116 to TAE40122 for existing holders.

**Other Notes:** Required under the Standards for RTOs 2015 to deliver/assess nationally recognised training.

**FAQs:**
- *I already hold TAE40116 — do I redo everything?* No — there's an upgrade pathway from TAE40116 to the current TAE40122.
