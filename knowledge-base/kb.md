## About FIT College
FIT College is an Australian Registered Training Organisation (RTO 31903)
delivering nationally recognised fitness, sport coaching, and related
qualifications. Courses are offered **online (virtual classroom)**, **on-campus
(face-to-face)**, or **blended**, with part-time and full-time options on the
fitness qualifications.

- **Phone:** 1300 887 017
- **Head office:** Maroochydore, QLD
- **Education support:** 7am–7pm, 7 days a week

## Courses & qualifications

### Fitness qualifications
- **Certificate III in Fitness (SIS30321)** — qualifies you to work as a gym
  instructor. Online or on-campus (full-time or part-time).
- **Certificate IV in Fitness (SIS40221)** — the personal trainer
  qualification. Online or on-campus (full-time or part-time).
- **Complete PT Program (Dual Certificate III & IV in Fitness)** — Cert III and
  Cert IV combined into one personal-training pathway. Online or face-to-face.

### Elite personal-training programs
- **FIT Elite Personal Trainer™ Program** — the Certificate III & IV pathway
  with added Elite inclusions. Online or face-to-face.
- **Fit Elite Ultra** — FIT College's most complete personal-training package.
  Online or face-to-face. Elite packages bundle additional included skillset
  courses.

### Sport & specialisations
- **Diploma of Sport – Coaching (SIS50321)** — online.
- **Strength & Conditioning (ASCA Level 1)** — online.
- **Individual Support – Disability Skillset** — online.

### Short courses / skillsets
- **Skillset short courses:** Kettlebell, Rumble, Stretch, Suspension —
  available online or face-to-face.
- **Provide CPR (HLTAID009)** — face-to-face.
- **Provide First Aid (HLTAID011)** — face-to-face.

### Training & assessment
- **Certificate IV in Training & Assessment (TAE)** — online or on-campus.

## Study modes
- **Virtual / online classroom** — enrol any time and start studying within 72
  hours.
- **Face-to-face full-time** — daytime/morning classes.
- **Face-to-face part-time** — evening classes.
- **Blended** — available as the student-visa study option.
- Students who start online can **upgrade to face-to-face** (a study-mode
  upgrade applies — the advisor explains the detail).

## Duration & study load
*Verified for the Certificate III in Fitness (SIS30321):*
- You have up to **12 months** to complete the qualification.
- **Virtual/online:** commonly completed in around **6 months** (≈27 hrs/week).
- **Face-to-face full-time:** about **7 weeks** at ~12 hrs/week.
- **Face-to-face part-time:** about **14 weeks** at ~6 hrs/week.

## Entry requirements
- **No formal entry requirements** for the Certificate III in Fitness.
- FIT College **does not accept students under 14 years of age**.
- Students need **language, literacy, numeracy and digital skills aligned to

## Intakes & enrolment
- **Face-to-face campuses:** three intakes a year — **February, May and
  September**.
- **Online/virtual:** **enrol any time**, start studying within 72 hours.

## Pricing & payment
- **Pricing varies by course, study mode (online vs face-to-face), and campus.**
  The advisor confirms an exact, current quote on the call — the chat does not
  quote prices.
- FIT College offers **flexible, interest-free payment plans**.
- **Government-subsidised funding** (e.g. the Certificate 3 Guarantee) may be
  available to eligible students — the advisor confirms eligibility. (Eligibility
  is limited; do not promise it.)
- If a prospect asks "how much," reassure them the advisor will walk through
  pricing, payment plans and any funding, and steer toward booking a quick call.
