## About FIT College

- FIT College is an Australian Registered Training Organisation (RTO) delivering nationally recognised fitness, sport-coaching and related qualifications.
- **RTO Number:** 31903
- **CRICOS Provider Code:** 03926G
- **ABN:** 51 143 802 966
- **Head office:** Suite 8, Level 1, 102 Wises Road, Maroochydore, QLD 4558
- **Phone:** 1300 887 017
- All qualifications are nationally recognised, fully accredited Australia-wide, and aligned with the Australian Qualifications Framework (AQF) Issuance Policy.
- Graduates can register with the Australian Register of Exercise Professionals (AusREPs) via AUSactive (the fitness industry peak body), with pathways to ICREPS and SMAANZ recognition.

## Courses

### SIS30321 Certificate III in Fitness — Gym Instructor
- **Study modes:** Online (virtual classroom) and face-to-face on campus; full-time or part-time.
- **Duration:**
  - Online: ~6 months common completion (12 months maximum allowable).
  - On-campus full-time: 7 weeks (~12 hrs/week).
  - On-campus part-time: 14 weeks (~6 hrs/week).
  - Student-visa blended: 22 weeks across 2 terms.
- **Entry requirements:** No formal entry requirements. Students must be 14 or older and meet language, literacy and numeracy (LLN) requirements.
- **What's included (8 topics):** First Aid; Exercise Anatomy & Physiology; Screening & Customer Service; Healthy Nutrition; Fitness Assessments; Design & Deliver Sessions; Group Exercise; Professional Development. Nominal hours: 1,242.
- **Support:** ~12 hours scheduled virtual support, 24/7 help requests, phone support 8am–6pm weekdays.
- **Career outcomes:** Gym Instructor, Group Fitness Instructor, Outdoor Group Fitness Instructor.

### SIS40221 Certificate IV in Fitness — Personal Trainer
- **Study modes:** Online (enrol anytime, start within 72 hours), face-to-face on campus, or blended; full-time, part-time and flexible options.
- **Duration:**
  - Online: ~6 months common completion (12 months maximum allowable).
  - On-campus full-time: 8 weeks.
  - On-campus part-time: 15 weeks.
  - Student-visa blended: 22 weeks (2 × 11-week terms).
- **Entry requirements / prerequisites:** Must already be competent in eight units, including HLTAID011 Provide First Aid (current or completed within the last three years). A Working with Children positive notification letter is also required.
- **What's included (9 topics):** movement assessment, personal-training delivery, nutrition, body composition, training adolescent/older clients, business proposals and a behaviour-change project. Knowledge quizzes, interactive online tasks and hands-on practical assessments. ~12 hours/week optional scheduled virtual classes, 24/7 communication-log support, phone support 8am–5pm Mon–Fri, 100% downloadable content.
- **Work placement:** No mandatory placement hours; assessments are tailored to the task.
- **Career outcomes:** Personal Trainer in commercial gyms/fitness centres, PT studios, outdoor group training, online/virtual coaching, corporate wellness, and sport & recreation centres.

### FIT Elite™ Personal Trainer
- A combined, industry-ready package built around the two core fitness qualifications:
  - SIS30321 Certificate III in Fitness, plus
  - SIS40221 Certificate IV in Fitness.
- Plus **30-CEC/CPD Advanced Skills** (Boxing, Suspension, Kettlebell training), and a choice of either an **ASCA Level 1 Accredited Strength & Conditioning stream** or an **NDIS Disability Skill Set (CHCSS00130)**.
- Available online via the FIT College learning platform. (Specific duration/price quoted by an advisor.)

### Strength & Conditioning Coach (ASCA Level 1 Accredited)
- **Accreditation:** ASCA Level 1 Strength & Conditioning Coach.
- **Covers:** designing/implementing training for strength, power, agility and endurance — exercise science, biomechanics, program design, fitness testing, coaching principles, athlete development.
- **Study mode:** Online; enrol anytime and start within 72 hours.
- **Duration:** ~6 months allowable (~300 nominal hours, ~12 hrs/week).
- **Entry requirements:** None; students must be 14 or older and meet LLN requirements.

### SIS50321 Diploma of Sport (Coaching)
- **Study modes:** Online/blended (6 hrs/week virtual classes + ~19 hrs/week self-study) or face-to-face (Gold Coast, Brisbane, Melbourne, Sydney).
- **Duration:** ~18 months common completion online (24 months maximum allowable); 44 weeks / 4 terms face-to-face. ~1,230 nominal hours (~25 hrs/week).
- **Entry requirements:** None; should demonstrate interest in sport coaching and meet LLN standards.
- **What's included (5 topics):** Strength & Conditioning; Sport Programs; Sport Integrity & Leadership; Sport Performance Coaching; First Aid.
- **Career outcomes:** Sport coach (with a club or independently), mentor, and sports-development roles in clubs, academies and national governing bodies.

## Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) & Credit Transfer

- **What it is:** An assessment-only pathway that formally recognises your existing skills, knowledge and workplace experience instead of teaching new material. It can recognise individual units, clusters of units, or a full qualification.
- **Credit Transfer:** prior formal training and qualifications can be credited toward a new qualification.
- **Fees:**
  - **Current FIT College students:** RPL is **free** for the qualification you're already enrolled in.
  - **Non-current students:** **$995** for one qualification review; **$1,495** for two qualification reviews. Any remaining "gap" study is charged per unit, or you can exit with a Statement of Attainment.
- **Process (4 steps):** 1) Application & preparation (resume, self-assessment, evidence checklist); 2) Evidence submission (uploaded electronically, mapped to competencies); 3) Evidence assessment (portfolio review plus interview/practical demonstration if needed); 4) Outcome — full RPL, partial RPL (with optional gap training), or a Statement of Attainment.
- **Evidence:** must show quality, authenticity and currency — preferably within 2 years, rarely accepted beyond 5 years.

## Refund & withdrawal policy

- **Cooling-off period:** 72 hours (including weekends/public holidays) from when the Confirmation of Registration is signed. It ends early if 72 hours pass, the student attends class, logs into the platform, or receives goods. A **$250 per-qualification** fee applies to a withdrawal made within this window.
- **Withdrawal after cooling-off:** an administration fee of **$250 per qualification** applies to approved withdrawals.
- **No refund is provided** if a student simply withdraws or changes their mind, fails to pay, has personal/financial changes, switches from face-to-face to online, doesn't finish within the term, has a visa refused due to non-start, or breaches the agreement.
- **Partial refunds may apply** for: a verified terminal illness or permanent disability diagnosed after registration; FIT College being unable to deliver the course (student then has two weeks to request a refund or a free course transfer); or overpayments.
- **Deferrals & suspensions:** deferrals must be submitted within the first 30 days before classes start; suspensions need 30 days' prior notice and aren't accepted in the final 30 days. Both are generally approved for up to three (3) months. An approved deferral/suspension does **not** automatically remove or change financial obligations.
- **Payment obligation:** the biller/guardian remains liable for the total purchased course cost regardless of completion.

## Frequently asked questions

**Are FIT College courses nationally recognised and accredited?**
Yes — all courses are nationally recognised and fully accredited Australia-wide. FIT College is RTO 31903 and aligns with the AQF Issuance Policy.

**How long do the on-campus courses run?**
Full-time on-campus is generally 14 weeks (Mon–Thu, 10:00am–1:00pm); part-time is 2 evenings per week (6:00pm–9:00pm) over 28 weeks. (Individual course durations are listed per course above.)

**How long do the online Cert III and Cert IV take?**
You have up to 12 months to complete each qualification. Many finish in around 6 months; you work at your own pace.

**Is online study equivalent to face-to-face?**
Yes — both deliver the same nationally regulated training package and the same qualification. Online means independent study through readings, videos and assessments via the online platform; no physical textbooks are needed.

**Is there practical work and work experience?**
Yes — assessments are hands-on. Students perform practical tasks (not just observe). Cert IV has no mandatory placement hours; assessments are tailored to the task.

**Am I too old to become a personal trainer?**
No age limit — students range from about 16 to 61.

**Are there extra costs?**
On-campus: only personal stationery and a device/internet. Online: you must complete First Aid certification at your own expense and have access to a local gym for practicals.

**What payment options are there?**
Individual, interest-free payment plans (direct debit) or pay-in-full. Minimum deposit $250 and minimum weekly repayment $75.

**Will I be job-ready, and is there work available?**
Courses are built to make graduates job-ready, and the industry continues to seek qualified, enthusiastic trainers. (Fitness work is often part-time, casual or self-employment-based — outcomes depend on effort and pathway; an advisor can talk through realistic options.)

**How does RPL work?**
Prior formal fitness education can be credited toward a new qualification. A careers advisor helps map a completion plan for any remaining units. (See the RPL section above for fees and process.)
