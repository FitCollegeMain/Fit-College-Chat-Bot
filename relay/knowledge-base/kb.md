## Qualifications offered
FIT College's fitness pathway is a simple two-step process: complete the Certificate III, then the Certificate IV, to become a fully qualified Personal Trainer.

### Certificate III in Fitness — SIS30321 (Fitness Instructor)
- Qualifies you to instruct group fitness classes as a **Fitness Instructor**.
- **15 units** · nominal hours **1242** · up to **12 months** allowable to complete.
- Delivery options:
  - **Face-to-face, Full time:** ~7 weeks delivery (~8 weeks to complete); day classes Mon–Thu 10am–1pm; intakes Feb · May · Sep.
  - **Face-to-face, Part time:** ~14 weeks delivery (~15 weeks to complete); night classes, evenings 6pm–9pm; intakes Feb · May · Sep.
  - **Online / blended (self-paced):** enrol anytime, start within 72 hours; ~15 hours/week self-study; ~6 months to complete.
- Units of competency: HLTAID011 Provide First Aid · SISFFIT047 Use anatomy & physiology knowledge for safe, effective exercise · SISFFIT032 Complete pre-exercise screening & service orientation · SISFFIT033 Complete client fitness assessments · SISFFIT040 Develop & instruct gym-based programs for individual clients · SISFFIT035 Plan group exercise sessions · SISFFIT036 Instruct group exercise sessions · SISFFIT052 Provide healthy eating information · SISXIND009 Respond to interpersonal conflict · SIRXSLS001 Sell to the retail customer · BSBOPS304 Deliver & monitor a service to customers · HLTWHS001 Participate in workplace health & safety · BSBPEF301 Organise personal work priorities · BSBCRT311 Apply critical thinking skills in a team environment · CHCPRP003 Reflect on & improve own professional practice.

### Certificate IV in Fitness — SIS40221 (Personal Trainer)
- Qualifies you to deliver personalised instruction & programs as a **Personal Trainer**.
- **17 units** · nominal hours **966** · up to **12 months** allowable to complete.
- Delivery options mirror the Certificate III:
  - **Face-to-face, Full time:** ~7 weeks delivery (~8 weeks to complete); day classes Mon–Thu 10am–1pm; intakes Feb · May · Sep.
  - **Face-to-face, Part time:** ~14 weeks delivery (~15 weeks to complete); evenings 6pm–9pm; intakes Feb · May · Sep.
  - **Online / blended (self-paced):** enrol anytime, start within 72 hours; ~15 hours/week self-study; ~6 months to complete.
- As part of this qualification, students complete a four-week **Behaviour Project Report** (from the Nutrition & Behaviour Change topic).
- Units of competency: SISFFIT041 Develop personalised exercise programs · SISFFIT042 Instruct personalised exercise sessions · SISFFIT034 Assess client movement & provide exercise advice · SISFFIT049 Use exercise science principles in instruction · SISFFIT043 Personalised programs for body-composition goals · SISFFIT044 Personalised programs for older clients · SISFFIT045 Personalised programs for adolescent clients · SISFFIT046 Plan & instruct online exercise sessions · SISFFIT050 Support exercise behaviour change · SISFFIT053 Support healthy eating for fitness clients · HLTPOP014 Assess readiness for & effect behaviour change · SISFFIT051 Establish & maintain professional practice · CHCCOM006 Establish & manage client relationships · BSBESB302 Develop and present business proposals · BSBESB301 Investigate business opportunities · HLTAID009 Provide cardiopulmonary resuscitation · CHCEDS040 Search & access online information.

### Fully qualified Personal Trainer
- Certificate III (15 units) + Certificate IV (17 units) = **32 total units** = fully qualified Personal Trainer.

### FIT Elite™ Package (premium)
The complete Personal Trainer qualification, plus advanced skills and a specialisation:
- **Complete PT:** Certificate III + Certificate IV.
- **Advanced Skills — 30 CPD included** (no extra study load): Boxing · Suspension Training · Kettlebell Training.
- **Choose one Specialisation:**
  - **S&C Coach** — Strength & Conditioning, includes **ASCA Level 1**; or
  - **Disability Skill Set** — Individual Support (CHCSS00130).
- The 30 CPD Advanced Skills and the Specialisation are what distinguish FIT Elite™ from the standard Complete PT package. To upgrade an enrolment, ask a Career Advisor.

## Study modes (per certificate)
- **Face-to-face — Part time:** 14 weeks/certificate; night classes; 2 sessions/week; 6pm–9pm; best for those working during the day.
- **Face-to-face — Full time:** 7 weeks/certificate; day classes; 4 sessions/week; 10am–1pm; fastest finish.
- **Self-paced — Online:** 12 months/certificate; online platform; you set the pace; start within 72 hours of enrolling; most flexible.
- All modes include scheduled weekly virtual class support, on-campus support, and phone support 9am–5pm Mon–Fri.

## Intakes
- **Face-to-face:** February · May · September.
- **Online:** start anytime (enrol any time; start within 72 hours).
